Translation and editorial language services

Iman Keyhanfard provides Persian-to-English and English-to-Persian translation informed by nearly two decades of English-language journalism and newsroom production. The focus is on media, current affairs and editorial content, where accurate meaning, context, names, titles and attribution matter as much as fluent wording.

News and current-affairs translation

News reports, official statements, press releases, political developments, background material and editorial copy, translated with attention to context and precise attribution.

Interview and documentary translation

Interview transcripts, questions and answers, documentary scripts, narration scripts and recorded statements, preserving the speaker’s meaning and tone.

Subtitles and captions

Persian and English subtitle translation, on-screen captions, speaker labels and time-coded text for news videos, interviews and documentary material.

Audio and video transcription

Persian or English speech transcription, interview transcription, speaker identification and timestamps when requested and supported by the source recording.

Proofreading and editorial review

Grammar, clarity, consistency, terminology, transliteration and style checks for translated or independently prepared media copy.

Bilingual content adaptation

Adapting wording for the intended audience and format while retaining the source meaning, rather than translating idioms mechanically.

How assignments are handled

  1. Brief and source: confirm language direction, subject, audience, format, deadline and reference material.
  2. Translation or transcription: preserve meaning, speaker intent, names, titles, figures and attribution.
  3. Editorial checks: review terminology and consistency; flag unclear or inaudible passages instead of guessing.
  4. Delivery: provide the agreed format, such as a clean document, time-coded transcript, subtitle file or caption text.

For subtitle work, the required file format, timing expectations, character limits and whether time-coding is needed should be agreed before the assignment begins.

Who this service is for

International news organizations, broadcasters, digital media outlets, documentary producers, journalists, researchers and organizations that need reliable Persian–English language support.
