Iman provides Persian-English and English-Persian translation for journalism, interviews, documentaries and media materials. Related services include transcription, subtitle and caption translation, proofreading, terminology consistency, speaker identification and timestamping when required. Names, titles and contextual references are checked carefully, and ambiguous source passages are flagged rather than silently guessed.
