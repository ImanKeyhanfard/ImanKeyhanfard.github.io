English Language Teaching & Conversation Practice

Practical English learning shaped by long-term teaching experience and professional use of English in journalism, media production and cross-cultural communication.

Learn English for communication, not only exercises

Iman has taught English since approximately 2006, including part-time university-level teaching, and has used English professionally in newsroom and international media environments. Lessons can focus on the learner’s actual goals: speaking with greater confidence, communicating at work, developing vocabulary or improving the clarity of everyday and professional English.

Sessions are planned around the learner’s current level, priorities and available time. The emphasis is on useful language, understandable explanations, active practice and feedback that can be applied between lessons.

Areas of support

  • Conversation practice: guided discussion, follow-up questions, fluency practice and strategies for expressing ideas more clearly.
  • Practical spoken English: everyday situations, travel, meetings, introductions, phone calls and workplace communication.
  • Vocabulary development: learning words and phrases in context, with attention to collocations and natural usage.
  • Grammar in use: explaining recurring errors and practicing structures through speaking and writing.
  • Professional communication: email wording, meeting language, presentations and interview practice, depending on the learner’s needs.
  • Pronunciation and listening: intelligibility, stress, connected speech and comprehension practice.

A practical lesson structure

  1. Needs discussion: clarify the learner’s goals, current level, interests and situations where English is needed.
  2. Focused practice: work on a manageable language target through examples, conversation, listening or short writing tasks.
  3. Feedback: identify useful corrections, explain why they matter and practice improved alternatives.
  4. Next steps: agree on a short independent task or topic to prepare before the next session.

Lesson length, frequency, online or in-person arrangements, materials and fees are discussed before a course begins. Progress depends on the learner’s starting point, consistency and practice outside lessons.

Who may benefit

Adult learners, university students, professionals, journalists and people who want practical English for everyday or workplace communication. A course can be adapted for different levels after an initial discussion.
