Voice and language support for media

Iman’s broadcast experience includes narration and voice-over work in an English-language television newsroom. This service brings together spoken-script preparation, Persian–English language support and production coordination. The precise role — voice performance, script adaptation, recording liaison or delivery review — is agreed for each project.

Recording facilities, additional voice talent and post-production can be coordinated when required, subject to availability, budget and client approval. This service does not imply ownership of a recording studio.

Available support

  • Persian narration and voice-over: spoken delivery for agreed scripts, explainers, reports and video content.
  • English narration support: English-language script preparation and voice-over coordination; performance requirements and sample review should be agreed before booking.
  • Script adaptation: making written copy sound natural when spoken while preserving the source meaning and editorial intent.
  • Pronunciation and terminology: checking names, titles, locations and specialist terms before recording.
  • Dubbing coordination: coordinating scripts, voice talent, recording schedules and handover requirements for Persian-language versions.
  • Subtitle and audio alignment: checking that spoken wording, translated text and captions remain consistent.
  • Recording and delivery liaison: confirming file format, sample rate, naming conventions, revision process and deadlines with the production team.

How a voice-over project works

  1. Share the script, reference video or brief, target language, audience and intended platform.
  2. Confirm pronunciation notes, tone, pace, duration, voice requirements and whether adaptation is needed.
  3. Agree who is recording, where the recording will take place, the delivery format and the revision scope.
  4. Review the agreed output for wording, pronunciation, completeness and technical delivery requirements.

For dubbing or timed narration, the client should provide the picture lock or current edit, timecodes and any required subtitle or cue sheet. Changes to the edit after recording may require a revised schedule and scope.

Deliverables and project details

  • Final narration or voice-over file, where performance is included in the agreed scope
  • Adapted spoken script with pronunciation notes
  • Recording brief or voice-talent instructions
  • Coordinated Persian dubbing or voice-over handover
  • Aligned script, transcript or subtitle text

Audio format, duration, number of revisions, usage rights, licensing, talent fees and any studio costs must be agreed before production. No broadcast usage rights or third-party talent rights are assumed unless expressly included.

Broadcasters, newsrooms, documentary producers, video agencies, educational publishers and communications teams producing Persian- or English-language content.
