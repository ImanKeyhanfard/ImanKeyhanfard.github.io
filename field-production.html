About this service

Iman Keyhanfard provides field-production and editorial coordination support for broadcasters, newsrooms and production companies working in Iran and the wider Middle East. Services can include story research, interview arrangements, local crew coordination, camera and sound logistics, location planning, shot lists, field notes, translation and organized footage handover. Assignments are scoped to the client's brief and may be delivered as local production support or as a coordinated video package.
