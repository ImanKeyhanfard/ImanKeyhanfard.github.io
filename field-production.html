Field Production & Video Production in Iran

Tehran-based editorial and field-production support for international broadcasters, newsrooms, documentary teams and digital publishers covering Iran and the wider Middle East.

Production support built around the editorial brief

Iman Keyhanfard combines nearly two decades of English-language newsroom experience with practical production coordination and regional knowledge. He helps international teams turn an editorial brief into a workable field plan, keeping communication between the commissioning editor, contributors and local production contacts clear and organized.

Assignments can be scoped as individual production tasks or as coordinated support across several stages. The exact role, responsibilities, approvals and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

What the assignment can include

  • Story preparation: background research, source mapping, interview research, timelines and editorial context for the proposed coverage.
  • Interview coordination: identifying potential contributors, making initial contact where appropriate, arranging schedules and confirming practical details. Participation remains subject to each contributor’s consent.
  • Field planning: location research, schedule planning, shot lists, crew calls, transport coordination and production notes.
  • Local crew liaison: communication with camera, sound and other production personnel, subject to availability, budget and client approval.
  • Editorial support: interview questions, briefing notes, translation, transcription coordination and clarification of names, titles and terminology.
  • Footage handover: organizing agreed files, shot descriptions, interview details and supporting notes for the receiving newsroom.

Typical deliverables

  • A concise production brief and proposed schedule
  • Interview contact and status sheet, where appropriate
  • Location notes, shot list and crew requirements
  • Background notes and verified source references
  • Translated interview material, captions or selected transcripts
  • An organized footage handover with agreed file descriptions

Deliverables depend on the assignment. Raw footage, edited packages, live reporting, camera equipment and additional crew are not assumed to be included unless explicitly agreed.

Workflow and editorial standards

  1. Brief: establish the story, intended audience, format, deadline, location and editorial requirements.
  2. Feasibility: identify practical constraints, contributor availability, crew needs, access requirements and dependencies.
  3. Plan: agree responsibilities, schedule, budget assumptions, communication and approval points.
  4. Produce: coordinate the agreed field tasks and keep the commissioning contact informed of material changes.
  5. Handover: deliver the agreed footage, notes, translations and production information in the requested format.

Names, titles, dates, figures and quotations should be checked against source material. Unconfirmed details and access limitations are flagged rather than presented as established facts.

Who this service is for

International news organizations, television and digital broadcasters, documentary producers, foreign correspondents, independent journalists and production companies that need a reliable local point of coordination for work in Iran.

Important operational boundaries

Access, filming permissions, contributor participation, travel conditions and third-party services depend on the relevant people, providers and authorities. No permission, interview, access outcome or security condition can be guaranteed. Crew, equipment and specialist services are confirmed only after availability and terms are checked.

Discuss a field assignment

When making an inquiry, include the story angle, proposed dates, locations, format, crew requirements, deadline and expected deliverables. Sensitive or confidential details can be discussed through an agreed secure channel rather than in an initial email.
