Media Production Coordination, Local Fixer & Project Logistics in Iran

Practical local coordination for international media and production teams working in Iran — connecting the editorial brief with schedules, people, locations and agreed production requirements.

A clear local coordination point

With a background in newsroom production and field coordination, Iman supports international clients who need help organizing the practical elements of a project in Iran. The role can involve communication between the commissioning team and local contacts, keeping requirements visible and helping the assignment move through agreed stages.

Every project is different. The scope is defined around the client’s brief, timeline, budget, locations and the tasks that can realistically be arranged. Coordination does not mean that every third-party service, permit or access request is under the coordinator’s control.

Coordination services

  • Project planning: task lists, schedules, milestones, contact sheets and practical production checklists.
  • Crew and supplier liaison: initial coordination with local camera, sound, transport, accommodation or other suppliers, subject to availability and client approval.
  • Interview and meeting logistics: proposed schedules, location details, communication and confirmation of practical arrangements.
  • Location and movement planning: routing and timing between agreed activities, with attention to realistic travel time and local constraints.
  • Visitor and team orientation: practical information about local working arrangements, communications and day-to-day logistics.
  • Project communication: status updates, issue escalation, changes to schedule and clear records of agreed decisions.
  • Administrative coordination: organizing non-legal project paperwork and communication where included in the brief.

What clients can expect

  • A written outline of tasks, responsibilities and dependencies
  • A proposed schedule and contact / coordination sheet
  • Updates on confirmations, pending items and changes
  • Coordination notes and agreed handover information
  • A clear distinction between confirmed arrangements and requests still awaiting approval

Third-party quotations, deposits, cancellation terms, insurance, permits and contractual commitments remain subject to the relevant provider, client and authority. No booking or commitment is made on a client’s behalf without explicit approval.

How coordination works

  1. Share the project purpose, dates, cities, team size and required support.
  2. Review feasibility, expected tasks, third-party dependencies and any known restrictions.
  3. Agree the scope, communication channel, approval process, fees and payment schedule.
  4. Coordinate the approved tasks and report changes promptly.
  5. Close the assignment with the agreed records, contacts and handover.

Who this service supports

Foreign correspondents, international broadcasters, documentary and film production teams, visiting researchers, media organizations and companies arranging a defined project in Iran.

Scope and limitations

This is a coordination service. It is not legal representation, immigration advice, a licensed travel-agency service, licensed tour guiding, security provision or a guarantee of official permission or access. Any regulated service must be handled by the appropriate qualified or licensed provider.
